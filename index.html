How it works

1. Purchase a license
Purchase one lifetime license for one Discord server.
2. Add WOS Voice Countdown
Authorize the bot for the Discord server where your Whiteout Survival community plays.
3. Open the control panel
Run /panel in your Discord server to open the countdown controls.
4. Join a voice channel
Enter your Discord voice channel and press Join.
5. Start your countdown
Select the countdown you need and WOS Voice Countdown will play it directly in your voice channel.

Each lifetime license is valid for one Discord server. Additional Discord servers require additional licenses.
